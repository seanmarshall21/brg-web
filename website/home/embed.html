Born in San Diego.
Built for community.

Blacktop Restaurant Group started with a simple belief: that a great meal is really just an excuse to connect. Since 2013, we've been growing a family of restaurant brands rooted in genuine hospitality, tasty food, and a deep love for the communities we call home.

We're not a corporation chasing locations — we're operators who eat at our own restaurants, know our guests by name, and measure success by how many times someone walks out the door smiling. Today, BRG operates 12 Board & Brew locations across San Diego and Orange County, plus Odie's Pizza Co. in Oceanside — and we're just getting started.
