Great food, great people, epic communities.

We're not just in these neighborhoods — we're part of them. From local sponsorships to giveback programs, showing up for our communities is just how we operate.

At BRG, community isn't a marketing initiative — it's in our DNA. When our restaurants thrive, the people around us should feel that too. That means showing up at local events, supporting causes that matter, partnering with schools and nonprofits, and investing in the places our guests call home.

How we give back

Urban Surf 4 Kids

Our flagship give-back partner. We help get kids with disabilities and those facing adversity out on the water — building confidence, connection, and stoke one wave at a time. It's exactly the kind of community-first mission we exist to support.

Other contributions

From youth sports sponsorships to school fundraisers, holiday meal drives, and first-responder appreciation — when a local cause needs a hand, our doors and our kitchens are open. Small gestures, done consistently, add up.

Local partnerships

We team up with neighborhood nonprofits, schools, and grassroots organizations across San Diego and Orange County — because the best way to strengthen a community is to build alongside the people already doing the work.
