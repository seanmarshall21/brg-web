At BRG, community isn't a marketing initiative — it's in our DNA. When our restaurants thrive, the people around us should feel that too. That means showing up at local events, supporting causes that matter, partnering with schools and nonprofits, and investing in the places our guests call home.

How we give back

Urban Surf 4 Kids

Our flagship give-back partner. We help get kids with disabilities and those facing adversity out on the water — building confidence, connection, and stoke one wave at a time.

Local partnerships

Schools, youth sports, neighborhood nonprofits — we show up where our guests already are, with food, sponsorship and hands on deck.

Other contributions

From fundraiser nights to donated meals, a lot of what we do never makes it to a press release — and that's rather the point.
