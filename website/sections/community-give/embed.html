Urban Surf 4 Kids

As our flagship give-back partner, Urban Surf 4 Kids provides foster youth and kids experiencing hardship out into the water with surf lessons. Together, we use the healing power of surf therapy to share confidence, connection, and pure local stoke.

Local partnerships

Schools, youth sports, neighborhood nonprofits — we show up where our guests already are, with food, sponsorship and hands on deck.

Community Fundraisers

From school dine-out events to youth team fundraisers, helping local groups raise money is a huge part of how we operate. We partner with neighborhood organizations across our shops to donate a portion of sales straight back into their programs, giving local causes a reliable place to hit their goals.
