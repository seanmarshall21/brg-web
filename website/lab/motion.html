01 — Marker underline

The comp's stroke under “YOUR DAY GREAT.” tapers at both ends and sweeps up slightly to the right. The question is which technique keeps that taper while still reading as drawn rather than slid in.

U1CSS blob, scaleX on Home today

What ships right now (home/embed.html:46). Uniform thickness, squared-off ends, and scaleX stretches the shape as it grows — reads like a bar sliding out, not a marker. Baseline for comparison.

making your day great.
U2SVG stroke draw pen, not marker

stroke-dasharray draw along a curved path. The motion is right — it genuinely draws L→R — but a stroke has one uniform width, so it reads like a ballpoint. No taper.

making your day great.
U3Brush fill + angled wipe ★ recommended

The mark is a filled shape — thin at the start, thick through the middle, thin at the tip — so the taper survives. It's revealed by a wipe whose leading edge is slanted ~5°, matching a chisel-tip marker. Shape stays put; only the reveal moves.

making your day great.
U4Double-stroke scribble busy at small sizes

Base stroke, then a lighter return pass at 55% — the way you'd actually underline something twice. Great on a big hero headline, muddy anywhere under ~2rem. Parked here in case you want it for one hero moment.

02 — Marker highlight

Three hero highlights in the comps — teal “FOR COMMUNITY”, yellow “WHAT WE'RE ABOUT”, pink “A DIFFERENT KIND” — all rotated with rough edges. The small teal labels (OUR VISION, LEADERSHIP…) are clean rects and want their own treatment.

H0Static on Home today

No animation at all (home/embed.html:21) — the block is just there on reveal. Baseline.

built for community.
H1Gradient background wipe survives line wrap

Animates background-size 0→100%. Cheapest, and the only variant that still works when the highlighted phrase wraps across two lines. Square edges, no rotation — honest but plain.

built for community.
H2Rotated block, angled wipe

A rotated div behind the text, revealed by the same slanted wipe as U3. Gets the tilt from the comps. Edges are still perfectly straight.

built for community.
H3Rough SVG block + angled wipe ★ recommended

Same wipe, but the block is an SVG with wobbled edges and uneven corners — which is what actually sells “drawn by hand” in the comps. Fires var(--h-delay) after the text so the marker lands on top of settled type instead of racing it.

built for community.

Same technique, checked against the light sections and the other two highlight colors from the comps.
