- DECISION: 2026-08-10 · **Cross-section bleed rule (stacking model).** Sections are independent stacked fragments; no element spans two. A design element that crosses a seam **lives in the LOWER (later) section and bleeds UPWARD** (`overflow:visible` + absolute, negative `top`) — later sections paint on top, so upward bleed shows; downward bleed gets covered by the lower section's bg and is disallowed. Ex: Careers surfer → owned by `careers-positions`, bleeds up over `careers-apply`. @Finn build to this.
  **EXCEPTION — shared chrome (footer/nav):** when the neighbor is the shared `[brg_footer]` (or `[brg_nav]`), ownership flips to the PAGE-SPECIFIC section, which bleeds DOWN (or up into nav) — the doodle can't live in the shared, page-agnostic chrome. To make the downward bleed paint over the footer's bg, give the owning section `position:relative; z-index:2; overflow:visible` and absolutely-position the doodle with negative `bottom`. Instance: Team `team-apply` BRG-cluster (`icons/icn-clust-all-trim.svg`) bleeds down into the footer → owned by `team-apply`. **Verify this bleed on the LIVE page, not just the harness — the Oxygen `ct-shortcode` wrapper (outside the harness) could clip it.** Export: whole doodle stays in the page-specific section's export (transparent), footer export stays clean. Sean is exporting per-section (transparent-bg) refs named to our section ids into `website/mocks/`; bleeding elements go in ONE export (the owner), not split. Doodles are placement-only refs — we already have the SVG icons; only export a doodle as SVG if it's not in `assets/media/icons`.
